# Junction 2026: Fact Sheet

*Checked against both official pages on Fri 13 Nov 2026, 14:50 Prague time (15:50 Finnish time). Every time below is Finnish time (EET, UTC+2), one hour ahead of Prague. The site says the schedule is preliminary and times may still shift.*

## Basics
- **Event:** Junction 2026, "European Tech Renaissance", a 48-hour, in-person hackathon
- **Dates:** Fri 13 to Sun 15 Nov 2026. Junction Week pre-events started Mon 9 Nov (no specific pre-events have been published).
- **Venue:** Hype Areena, Vanha Jorvaksentie, 02320 Espoo (Kivenlahti). That's Espoo, not Helsinki.
- **Getting there:** Western end of the Helsinki metro line, about 30 min by metro from Helsinki Central. By car about 20 min from central Helsinki and about 30 min from Helsinki-Vantaa Airport.
- **Scale:** About 2,000 builders from 100+ countries
- **Cost:** Free for accepted participants. The only paid ticket is the Investor Pass.

## Teams & who can join
- **Team size:** 1 to 4 people. Teams can form on site before hacking starts.
- **Open to:** "Anyone who builds", from any background or experience level, including students.
- **Applications:** Closed at midnight on 25 Oct 2026, GMT+3.

## Age and minors ⚠️
- The site only says "**Participants are mainly 18 and over.**"
- **Not published anywhere official:** a minimum age, a parental consent form, or any rules for under-18s. Anyone under 18 should have school or parent consent paperwork on hand and ask staff at registration.
- The event follows the **MLH Code of Conduct** (linked in the site footer).

## Judging
1. **Partner tracks:** each Challenge Partner picks its own winners against its own criteria. **Those criteria aren't published** (Cala will explain theirs at the Saturday 11:00 workshop).
2. **Top 5 peer review:** the Main Event winner is chosen first through face-to-face peer review, which picks the top 5.
3. **Live pitch and grand jury:** the top 5 pitch live, and a seven-judge grand jury picks the overall winner.

**Grand jury, with what each says they look for:**
- **Kia Kanninen** (CEO, Founders House Helsinki): teams that start from a problem worth solving, not just a feature that's easy to build.
- **Daksh Raghuvanshi** (RL Lead, AfterQuery): teams that can explain exactly why an agent fails in their world, and whose reward holds when someone tries to break it.
- **Otto Söderlund** (AI Lead, Supercell): a real user problem, and something people want to come back to.
- **Timo Ahopelto** (Managing Partner, Lifeline Ventures): nothing stated.
- **Umar Noor** (Developer Experience, Google DeepMind): nothing stated.
- **Hubertus "Beer" van der Drift** (CTO, Context.dev): teams that move fast, stay pragmatic, and find the one unique insight that solves a problem 10x faster or better.
- **Arturs Polis** (CTO, Verda): teams that tackle the hardest challenges, with a prototype that has a clear path to production and scale.

## Submission
- **Platform:** the [Junction Platform](https://hackjunction.app/hackathons/junction-2026). **It's not Devpost.**
- **Deadlines:** preliminary submission Fri 23:59 (or 23:55, see above), submissions open Sat 12:00, final submission Sun 10:00.
- **License and ownership:** your team owns what it builds, but submissions are **open-sourced under the MIT licence**, with your team credited.
- **Unknown:** the required fields, the repo, demo video or slide requirements, video length, and what counts as a valid preliminary submission. None of this is on the public pages yet. Check the Platform's submission form right after registration.

## Logistics
- **Food:** dinner Fri 19:30, lunch Sat 13:00, dinner Sat 18:00, all on the 2nd and 3rd floor. **No Sunday meals, no breakfasts and no menu are listed.** Bring snacks for Sunday morning.
- **Sleeping:** the venue is open around the clock for all 48 hours, but there are **no dedicated sleeping areas**. Junction recommends a nearby room.
- **Wi-Fi:** not published. Bring a phone hotspot as a backup.
- **What to bring:** no official packing list. Unofficial suggestions: laptop and charger, EU plug extension or power strip, phone and charger, ID, water bottle, Sunday snacks, a hoodie, a power bank. For hardware, bring your own cables and a USB hub.
- **Travel grants:** closed. Partner hotels offer discounted rooms.
